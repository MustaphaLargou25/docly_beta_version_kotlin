# Docly 🎓
> *By students. For students.*

Docly is a native Android platform designed for Moroccan and Francophone university students to share, discover, and collaborate on academic resources. 

## 🚀 Features

* 📚 **Resource Sharing**: Upload, organize, and discover course modules, summaries, and past exams.
* 🏆 **Points System**: Gamified learning environment! Earn points for uploading documents, climb the leaderboard, and use your points to unlock exclusive materials.
* 🔍 **Smart Search**: Browse content specifically tailored to your University, Field of Study, and Semester.
* 💬 **Community Hub**: A dedicated social feed to ask questions, share answers, and directly message peers from your program.
* 🌍 **Multilingual**: Out-of-the-box support for Arabic (with full RTL support), French, and English.

## 🎨 Design & UI

Built with a custom **Geometric Balance** theme, Docly provides a focused, clean, and highly readable experience:
* **Modern Aesthetic**: Features an elegant `PrimaryBlue` (#2563EB) color palette with soft gradients and crisp container borders.
* **Material 3**: Leverages Jetpack Compose M3 capabilities for smooth navigation, pill-shaped interactive elements, and accessible touch targets.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Platform**: Native Android
* **Language**: [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
* **UI Toolkit**: [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose)
* **Routing**: Jetpack Navigation Compose
* **Architecture**: Modern Android Architecture

## 📱 Core Screens
1. **Home/Search**: Personalized dashboard showing your specific university modules, stats, and recent community activity.
2. **Library**: Manage your downloaded, saved, and uploaded PDF documents.
3. **Community Feed**: Connect with peers, post questions, and share insights.
4. **Profile**: Track your current points, ranking, user statistics, and language preferences.
